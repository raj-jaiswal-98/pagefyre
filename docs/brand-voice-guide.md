# PageFyre — Brand Voice & Editorial Tone Guide

> **Core Tagline**: *"Here be dragons. On any webpage."*  
> **Brand Persona**: The mischievous medieval archivist who found an arcade cabinet inside an illuminated grimoire.

---

## 🌟 The Three Pillars of Voice

### 1. Playful, Never Destructive
- **Mindset**: We are playing with the internet, not breaking it. Chaos is an art form and a source of delight, not malicious griefing.
- **Word Choice**: *Unleash, ignite, devour, frolic, carve, summon, dance, awaken.*
- **Avoid**: Words with morbid, violent, or grimdark connotations (*kill, murder, gore, slaughter, trash, execute*).

### 2. Mythic & Grimoire-Infused
- **Mindset**: Speak like an ancient tome inscribed with enchanted gold ink, but delivered with the snappy brevity of an arcade prompt.
- **Word Choice**: *Grimoire, runes, bestiary, reality, arcane, celestial, sigils, invocations, vellum, brass.*
- **Avoid**: Generic tech jargon (*"Delete all divs"*, *"Clear cache"*, *"Reset UI"*). We don't clear the cache—we *"Restore Reality"*.

### 3. Mischievous & Confident
- **Mindset**: A wry wink to the reader. Websites can be stiff, corporate, and stuffy; PageFyre is the impish dragon perched on the margin, ready to torch the boring parts with a grin.
- **Word Choice**: *Mischief, havoc, swarm, behold, dare, claim.*

## 🛡️ Legal & Brand-Safety Guardrails
1. **Never reference copyrighted IP**:
   - ❌ *Darth Vader, Star Wars, Godzilla, Kong, Iron Man, Spider-Man, Avengers.*
   - ✅ *Ignis (Pyre Drake), Lord Umbra (Shadow Sovereign), Tectonus (Primal Behemoth), Mecha Vanguard (Runic Automaton), The Void Maw (Eldritch Leviathan).*
2. **Never claim irreversible destruction**:
   - Always assure users that changes are strictly client-side, temporary, and 100% reversible.
