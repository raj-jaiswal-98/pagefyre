# Privacy Policy for PageFyre

**Last Updated:** October 2026

PageFyre ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for the PageFyre Chrome Extension.

---

### 1. No Data Collection

**PageFyre does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data.**

Specifically, we do NOT collect:
- Personally Identifiable Information (name, email, address, phone number)
- Health, financial, or authentication credentials
- Web browsing history, bookmarks, or URLs visited
- User activity, keystrokes, mouse logs, or network traffic
- Content of the websites you visit

---

### 2. How Permissions Are Used

PageFyre requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function as an interactive on-screen entertainment overlay:

- **`activeTab`**: Used solely when you click the PageFyre toolbar icon to mount the temporary game canvas and tactical HUD on the currently open tab. It operates strictly in memory and does not track your tab history.
- **`storage`**: Used solely to persist your local user preferences (sound volume level, audio mute status, and dark/parchment theme choice) on your local device. No data is sent off your device.
- **`scripting`**: Used to execute the extension's bundled local scripts and CSS into the active tab on-demand when you invoke a game action.

---

### 3. Local Execution & No Remote Code

- All code, scripts, sounds, and graphics are bundled locally within the extension package.
- All sound effects are synthesized on the fly via the browser's native Web Audio API; no external media files or remote servers are contacted.
- PageFyre does not use third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no telemetry trackers), external CDNs, or advertising networks.

---

### 4. Non-Destructive Operation

PageFyre is an entertainment overlay. Visual effects exist strictly within a client-side HTML5 canvas layer. Clicking "Restore Reality" or refreshing the tab immediately returns the webpage to its original, unaltered state.

---

### 5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties under any circumstances.
